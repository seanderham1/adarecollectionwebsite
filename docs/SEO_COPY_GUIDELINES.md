# SEO copy guidelines (The Adare Collection)

Approved principles for site copy aligned with the May 2026 SEO strategy and legal positioning.

## Brand & independence

- The Adare Collection Limited is an **independent** accommodation provider (see footer disclaimer).
- Do **not** imply affiliation with Adare Manor, Ryder Cup Europe, or official ticket sales.
- **Estate positioning**: use “on the Adare Manor demesne / within the gated resort” where factually true for a given property—not “official Ryder Cup accommodation” unless legal confirms.

## “Official” language

- Acceptable: “official Park & Ride” (where used in property copy), “official Ryder Cup ticket information” linking to rydercup.com.
- Avoid: “official Ryder Cup accommodation provider” or “official packages.”

## Packages & pricing

- Ryder Cup 2027 **packages** mean curated **estate stays + optional services** (chef, chauffeur, concierge), confirmed enquiry-by-enquiry.
- “Packages for sale” / pricing: **price on application**; no fixed public price lists unless approved.

## Travel services (`/services`)

- Target **estate-hosted services**: private chef, chauffeur, concierge, ground transfers, helicopter **subject to availability**.
- **Do not** sell or imply booking of flights, airline packages, or third-party package holidays (see FAQ).

## Helicopter & chauffeur

- Always qualify helicopter as **subject to availability** and arranged in advance.
